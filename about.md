# about

<!-- ![A picture of Maiyo's face](/assets/maiyo_face.jpg) -->

Maiyo (“my-oh”) Saji (they/she) was born and raised in Morningside Heights, Manhattan. They are second-generation Japanese American and have worked as a facilitator, community organizer, and litigation assistant on a variety of issues related to environmental and racial justice, immigrant rights, and climate and energy justice policies. They are also an oil painter, printmaker, and more recently learning to bookbind. 

Currently, Maiyo is a master’s student in Human Geography at the University of Minnesota. 

Their research interests include:

- Geovisualization in climate technologies (climate simulations, digital twinning, vulnerability mapping)
- Decolonial anthropocene studies
- Critical Political Ecology
- Digital Natures
- Visual culture and aesthetic theory
- New Media 

Maiyo holds a B.A. from Brown University in Ethnic Studies. You can find their undergraduate academic portfolio [here](https://brown.digication.com/mayo-saji-capstone-ethnic-studies-2020/home).
